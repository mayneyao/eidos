**Eidos Sync** is an optional cloud synchronization service built for users who regularly switch between multiple computers (such as an office desktop and a personal laptop).

Built strictly on local-first principles: your files remain fully functional offline on every machine. Whenever connected to the internet, your files, edits, and version history mirror silently in the background.

---

## 1. Enable Sync in Three Steps

1. **Sign In**: Open Settings from the bottom sidebar in Eidos Lite and sign into your Eidos account.
2. **Open Sync Panel**: Click the **Sync** button (cloud icon) in the top-right corner of the window.
3. **Turn on Sync**: Click **Enable Sync**. Eidos Lite automatically creates a private, encrypted sync channel for your Space in the cloud.

Once active, the cloud icon subtly animates during sync activity and displays a green ready badge when your local files match the cloud.

---

## 2. Continuing Work on a Second Machine

On your second computer (macOS, Windows, or Linux):

1. Install Eidos Lite and sign in with the exact same Eidos account.
2. On the welcome screen or file menu, choose **Sync existing Space from cloud**.
3. All `.eidos` tables, Markdown notes, attachments, and full version timelines will download automatically. Edits on either device will now stay continuously synchronized.

---

## 3. Working Offline & Safe Conflict Resolution

- **Seamless Offline Work**: When traveling or without WiFi, continue creating and editing your documents normally. The offline experience is identical to online.
- **Automatic Catch-up**: As soon as internet connectivity returns, Eidos Lite smoothly reconciles and pushes your local changes.
- **Smart Conflict Handling**: If two computers edit the exact same document while disconnected, Eidos never blindly overwrites your work. The Sync panel will prompt you to **Review changes**, letting you inspect both sides and choose which updates to keep.

---

## 4. Storage Quotas & Service Plans

- Using Eidos Lite locally is completely free and unconstrained.
- Sync provides dedicated high-speed incremental cloud storage. You can activate Sync via the [Account Dashboard](https://eidos.space/account) or the [Pricing Page](https://eidos.space/pricing).
- If your Sync subscription ever lapses, **your local files, tables, and version histories remain completely safe and accessible on your local hard drive**.
