Sync maintains a local copy of a Space on each device.

| Connection    | Requirements                                                        | Where changes travel                                        |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| LAN sync      | Eidos Lite on a computer and a mobile app on the same local network | Directly between paired devices. No cloud account required  |
| Eidos Sync    | An account with Sync access                                         | Through the official service with end-to-end encryption     |
| Custom server | Spark activated on each computer and a compatible Graft server      | Directly through your own server with end-to-end encryption |

Local files remain usable offline. Open **Sync** to see this Space's current
status and next action. Choose **Cloud sync** or **LAN sync** in the sidebar.
Under **Cloud sync**, use **Server** to choose Eidos Sync or a saved server.
A connected Space shows its server in the panel. Open **⋯** for connection
options, including server management.

A Space uses one cloud service at a time. LAN sharing is independent: it can
remain enabled alongside either cloud service, or without a cloud connection.
After setup, **Use on another device** guides you through opening the existing
connection on another computer. Open **Sync settings** in the bottom menu for the current
connection to manage its account, storage, and disconnect actions. Use
**Manage servers** to add or edit custom server configurations.

**Versions** owns local changes, saved versions, and version history. The Sync
sidebar shows connection and transfer status, upload and receive actions, and
any conflict or recovery steps that need your attention.

This guide explains LAN sync first.

## Pair your phone and computer

1. Connect both devices to the same local network, such as your home Wi-Fi. Keep Eidos Lite running and the mobile app open in the foreground. Allow local network permissions when prompted.
2. On the computer, open the Space's **Sync** panel and select **LAN sync**. Enable the computer's LAN service if prompted.
3. Choose to pair a new device and display its pairing code. You can also pair without an open Space under **Settings → Devices**.
4. On the phone, open **Sync** and select **Scan QR Code**. You can also choose **Use pairing code** and enter the code manually.
5. When the phone shows **Waiting for approval**, check the device name and accept the request on the computer.

If the approval dialog is in the background, open Eidos Lite through its notification.
Pairing codes expire after five minutes.
Generate a new code if the request expires or fails.

Pairing grants access only to the Spaces you share explicitly.
Transfers use encrypted connections tied to the paired device identity.

## Make a Space available and download it

1. Open the Space in Eidos Lite.
2. Open **Sync → LAN sync** and share this Space with the paired devices when prompted.
3. On the phone, open **Sync** and select the paired computer. Under **This computer's Spaces**, select **Download** beside the Space.
4. Wait for the initial download to finish. The app adds the Space to **Files**, where you can use it offline.
5. Open a known note or record and check an attachment before you start work.

The phone stores a separate local copy.
An interrupted first download does not show as a ready Space.
Retry the download from the Sync page.
To share another Space, enable it on the computer and download it on your phone.
You do not need to pair the devices again.

Some transferred files require desktop features.
The phone preserves these files even when it cannot open or edit them.

A shared Space remains available while Eidos Lite runs in the background.
Closing the application stops sharing.
When you restart Eidos Lite, it restores the enabled LAN service and available
shared Spaces. Explicitly stopping the LAN service disables that restoration
until you enable it again. The application preserves your authorized device list.

## Synchronize changes in both directions

You start LAN sync manually from the phone.
Saving locally does not send edits to the other device automatically.

1. Finish your edits and save the file.
2. On the phone, open **Sync**, select the computer, and tap **Sync** beside the Space.
3. Keep both devices connected until the sync finishes. Check the progress indicator and last sync time shown by the app.
4. Open the changed file on the other device to check its contents.

For your first test, edit a note on your computer.
Start a sync from your phone and check the note there.
Then edit the note on your phone, sync again, and check the note on your computer.
The online indicator only reports network connectivity.
Wait for the sync progress to complete before you edit files again.

## Offline work, interruptions, and conflicts

You can edit downloaded files offline.
When both devices connect to the local network again, start a sync from the phone.
Select **Stop sync** to cancel an active transfer.
Do not delete the local Space to resolve transfer issues.

The computer merges non-conflicting edits automatically.
If both devices edited the same record, the phone keeps its local files.
The phone prompts you to resolve the conflict in the computer's **Sync** panel.
Resolve the conflict on the computer, then sync again from the phone.
Edits made on your phone while waiting are included in the next sync.

For connection errors, see [LAN troubleshooting](/user-guide/troubleshooting/#lan-sync-cannot-connect-or-find-a-space).

## Stop sharing or unpair a device

- Open **Sync → LAN sync** and stop sharing to make only this Space unavailable.
- Turn off **LAN Service** in **Settings → Devices** to stop all sharing. Device pairings remain saved.
- Select **Unpair** on either device to revoke access. Unpairing preserves downloaded files and history. Reconnecting requires a new pairing process.

## Use cloud sync

Official Eidos Sync uses end-to-end encryption to connect separate local copies
through your Eidos account. You need an account with Sync access. Your files remain
available locally while offline.

Cloud Sync is available in Eidos Lite on macOS, Windows, and Linux.

### Connect the first computer

1. Open the folder you want to sync in Eidos Lite.
2. Open **Sync → Cloud sync** and select **Eidos Sync**, then sign in with an account that can upload to Sync. If prompted, select **Enable local versions** to prepare this Space without leaving setup.
3. Check the file count, size, and excluded files.
4. If asked, create and confirm a **Sync password**. Store it in your password manager: you will need the same password on another computer. This is separate from your Eidos account password.
5. Select **Connect this Space** and wait for the first upload to finish. The panel returns to the current Space's sync status.

While the first sync runs, setup is replaced by its current stage. You can close
the panel and keep working; reopening it shows the same task. Expand **Sync
details** for transfer information. If the connection fails, the panel shows the
reason and the next recovery action.

For an already connected Space, select the other service under **Cloud sync**
to configure it. The current connection remains identified until you confirm
connecting the new service.

Lite can remember the password on macOS and on Linux with a supported system
keyring. Windows, or an environment where the password cannot be saved, asks
for it again after restarting. The password is never sent to the Sync service.

Hidden files and filenames such as `secret-garden.jpg` are ordinary files.
They do not require a separate confirmation. The file check blocks unsupported
entries, symbolic links, and individual files larger than 1 GiB. Ignore rules
exclude files that have not entered version history; adding an existing tracked
file to `.graftignore` does not remove its saved history or stop its inclusion.

### Open the Space on another computer

On the first computer, **Sync → Cloud sync → Use on another device**
shows the steps for its current connection; this does not change the service.

1. In Eidos Lite, sign in to the same Eidos account.
2. Open the Space menu and choose **Open Synced Space**, then select the cloud Space.
3. Enter that Space's sync password. If you have already saved the same password under **Settings → Account & Services → Sync**, Lite can use it. Choose **Enter a different sync password** for a Space with another password.
4. Choose a new destination folder and wait for the download to finish.
5. Open a known record and attachment to check the restored copy before editing.

A wrong password does not publish an incomplete local Space. Retry with the
password used when that Space was connected. Signing in again or resetting the
account password does not change the sync password.

### Send and receive changes

During cloud uploads and downloads, Lite shows transferred bytes. When the
transfer total is known, it also shows a percentage; preparation and other
stages without a known total use an activity indicator. A transfer reaching
100% does not mean Sync has finished: verification and local file preparation
may still follow. Wait for Sync to finish before closing the app.

Saving a file changes the local copy. Open **Versions**, review and select the
changed files, then choose **Save selected files** to record them in local
history. Return to **Sync → Cloud sync** and upload the saved versions.
Background checks look for remote updates and do not upload changes.

On the other computer, select **Sync now** when the panel says versions are up
to date. Lite checks the remote and receives or uploads saved versions when
needed. Otherwise, select **Check remote updates**, then receive the versions
offered by the panel. Save local changes as a version before receiving.
An offline or unchecked Space is not proof
that the two copies match; wait for the panel to report that the versions are up
to date.

If both computers have different saved changes, Sync asks you to review a merge.
Review the text or record choices and save the result, then upload it. For a
binary file, choose the version to keep or preserve separate copies. Do not
delete one Space to dismiss a conflict. See [history and recovery](/user-guide/history-and-recovery/)
for retaining local work.

### Disconnect cloud sync

In the Space's **Sync → Cloud sync** panel, open **Sync settings** beside the current connection, choose
**Disconnect cloud sync**, and confirm.
This removes this computer's connection, including pending background checks. Local files,
unsaved edits, and Graft history stay in the same folder. The cloud copy and connections
on other computers are not deleted.

You can disconnect while offline, signed out, or after deleting the cloud repository.
To create another cloud copy, enable Sync again in the same folder. Lite
uploads the locally saved history using your sync password.
No folder copying or renaming is needed. Other computers must open the new cloud Space;
they do not switch connections automatically.

### Passwords and recovery

The password in **Settings → Account & Services → Sync** is the default for new
Spaces on this computer. Each Space has its own encryption key. Changing this
setting affects new connections; it does **not** change the password of an
existing cloud Space. Lite does not currently offer a per-Space password-change
action. Keep the passwords used by existing Spaces when changing the default.

If you forget a sync password, files already downloaded to a computer remain
readable and can be backed up. A computer that still remembers the password may
continue syncing. Preserve that computer's local files before reinstalling the
app or removing its data. Eidos cannot recover the password or decrypt the cloud
copy for you. If no usable local copy or password remains, resetting your account
password will not recover the encrypted contents.

Signing out of Eidos stops official Sync access and clears its active unlocked
session. It does not delete local files or disconnect custom servers. Quit Lite
to clear active Sync sessions for all services.
When Sync needs to be unlocked again, Lite can use the securely saved password
or ask you to enter it. End-to-end encryption protects the cloud copy; it does
not encrypt the local folder, SQLite files, or attachments. Use your operating
system's disk encryption to protect a lost computer.

### What the service can see

File contents, file paths, saved version messages, and branch names are encrypted
before upload. The service still knows the account and the cloud Space's display
name, repository identity, stored byte counts, request times, and opaque
relationships between saved versions. Avoid putting confidential information in
the cloud display name. Encryption does not hide all activity or replace an
independent backup.

The Sync service does not receive your decryption keys and cannot read the
encrypted contents. This protection does not extend to content you separately
send to Eidos Publish.

Cloud storage includes saved history and encrypted storage overhead, so it can
exceed the size of the current local folder. Deleting a current file does not
immediately remove its historical copies or free the associated quota. Check
your [account dashboard](https://eidos.space/account) for storage and access
limits. If uploads are blocked by quota or account access, keep working locally
and resolve the limit before retrying.

### Delete a cloud repository

Open [Account → Sync → Repositories](https://eidos.space/account?tab=sync&view=repositories).
Check the repository name and creation date, select **Delete**, and confirm the
permanent deletion. The page shows **Deleting…** until cloud cleanup finishes;
refresh the page if cleanup continues in the background. Storage usage updates
after cleanup completes.

Deleting a repository permanently removes all of its cloud files and saved
history. Local files and local history remain on each computer, including copies
already downloaded on other devices. The deleted connection can no longer
upload or download. Back up any local copy you want to keep before removing it
from your computer. Deleting a repository does not cancel your subscription.

## Use a custom server

Custom-server connections are a Spark feature in Eidos Lite on macOS, Windows,
and Linux. Each computer uses its existing Spark activation; the server does
not use a device seat. No official Sync subscription is required.

Use the [personal Graft Cloudflare template](https://github.com/eidos-space/graft-cloudflare)
or a server compatible with its discovery and repository management API. Lite
connects only to end-to-end encrypted repositories. A general Graft protocol
endpoint does not necessarily provide the listing and creation APIs Lite needs.

### Add your server

1. Activate Spark in **Settings → Account & Services** on this computer. If Spark
   is already active but the new feature is locked, choose **Refresh Spark benefits**.
2. Deploy your server and keep its HTTPS address and access token in your password manager.
3. Enable local version history if needed, and save the changes you want to upload.
4. Open **Settings → Account & Services → Sync → Add server**. Enter a name,
   the **Server address** and **Access token**, then choose **Test and save**.
   Saving validates access and stores the configuration on this computer.
5. Return to Sync and select the saved server under **Server**, or use **Switch
   server** for a connected Space. Set a sync password in Sync settings if
   needed, then choose **Connect** or review the switch.
   Wait for the encrypted upload and verification.

Selecting another cloud service or saving server settings keeps the existing
connection active. **Manage servers** also lets you edit a name, replace an access
token, or remove a saved configuration. Removing a configuration does not delete
remote data. Add a separate server when changing the address.

The server access token authorizes access; the sync password decrypts your
Space. They are separate secrets. Lite does not send the sync password to the
server, or send your server token to the Eidos account service. HTTPS is required.

The personal template uses one token for all of its repositories and devices.
Changing that token requires updating it on each computer, but does not change
your Space's encryption password or identity.

### Download on another computer

Activate Spark on the other computer, then add the same server address and
access token. Select the encrypted Space and choose **Download**. Enter the
Space's sync password, choose a new destination folder, and wait for the download
to finish. Check a known record and attachment before editing.

Use the same review, upload, receive, and conflict-resolution steps described
above. Each Space has one cloud connection at a time; different Spaces can use
different servers.

### Authorization and changing services

An existing Spark owner refreshes benefits once to receive this feature.
The signed device license can then be verified offline, including after signing
out of the Eidos account. Normal custom-server sync connects directly to your
server. Releasing Spark on this computer stops further custom-server operations
without deleting local files, history, or remote data. You can still disconnect.

To change the service used by a Space, keep a complete local copy and its history.
Open **Sync → Cloud sync → ⋯ → Switch server** and select the target service.
Configure the target service, select **Review switch**, and confirm the switch. Lite
creates a new encrypted repository and uploads the saved history. Do not replace
the old remote URL or copy encrypted server objects into a different repository.
Download and check the new copy on another computer before deleting the old
server copy. Other computers must connect to the new repository themselves.
The current connection remains identified separately from the target. Canceling
keeps it unchanged. If the upload fails, Lite restores the previous connection;
you may need to unlock it if its password was not saved on this device. After an
interrupted switch, reopen the Space to resume local connection recovery.

Your hosting provider's storage and request limits apply. Official Sync account
quotas and the Eidos account's repository deletion page do not manage your custom
server. Follow the server's own operations guide and keep independent backups.

## Keep a separate backup

Sync transfers edits and deletions to other devices.
Always maintain an independent [backup](/user-guide/data-safety/).
