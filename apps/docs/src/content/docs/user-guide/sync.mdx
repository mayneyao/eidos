Sync maintains a local copy of a Space on each device.

| Connection | Requirements | Where changes travel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LAN sync | Eidos Lite on a computer and a mobile app on the same local network | Directly between paired devices. No cloud account required |
| Cloud sync | An account and an active subscription plan | Through the cloud service |

Local files remain usable offline.
This guide explains LAN sync first.

## Pair your phone and computer

1. Connect both devices to the same local network, such as your home Wi-Fi. Keep Eidos Lite running and the mobile app open in the foreground. Allow local network permissions when prompted.
2. On the computer, open **Settings → Devices** and turn on **LAN Service**. Pairing does not require an open Space.
3. Select **Show pairing code**.
4. On the phone, open **Sync** and select **Scan QR Code**. You can also choose **Use pairing code** and enter the code manually.
5. When the phone shows **Waiting for approval**, check the device name and accept the request on the computer.

If the approval dialog is in the background, open Eidos Lite through its notification.
Pairing codes expire after five minutes.
Generate a new code if the request expires or fails.

Pairing grants access only to the Spaces you share explicitly.
Transfers use encrypted connections tied to the paired device identity.

## Make a Space available and download it

1. Open the Space in Eidos Lite.
2. Open **Sync → LAN Sync** and enable LAN sync for this Space.
3. On the phone, open **Sync** and select the paired computer. Under **This computer's Spaces**, select **Download** beside the Space.
4. Wait for the initial download to finish. The app adds the Space to **Files**, where you can use it offline.
5. Open a known note or record and check an attachment before you start work.

The phone stores a separate local copy.
An interrupted first download does not show as a ready Space.
Retry the download from the Sync page.
To share another Space, enable it on the computer and download it on your phone.
You do not need to pair the devices again.

Some transferred files require desktop features.
The phone preserves these files even when it cannot open or edit them.

A shared Space remains available while Eidos Lite runs in the background.
Closing the application stops sharing.
When you restart Eidos Lite, turn the LAN service and Space sharing back on.
The application preserves your authorized device list.

## Synchronize changes in both directions

You start LAN sync manually from the phone.
Saving locally does not send edits to the other device automatically.

1. Finish your edits and save the file.
2. On the phone, open **Sync**, select the computer, and tap **Sync** beside the Space.
3. Keep both devices connected until the sync finishes. Check the progress indicator and last sync time shown by the app.
4. Open the changed file on the other device to check its contents.

For your first test, edit a note on your computer.
Start a sync from your phone and check the note there.
Then edit the note on your phone, sync again, and check the note on your computer.
The online indicator only reports network connectivity.
Wait for the sync progress to complete before you edit files again.

## Offline work, interruptions, and conflicts

You can edit downloaded files offline.
When both devices connect to the local network again, start a sync from the phone.
Select **Stop sync** to cancel an active transfer.
Do not delete the local Space to resolve transfer issues.

The computer merges non-conflicting edits automatically.
If both devices edited the same record, the phone keeps its local files.
The phone prompts you to resolve the conflict in the computer's **Sync** panel.
Resolve the conflict on the computer, then sync again from the phone.
Edits made on your phone while waiting are included in the next sync.

For connection errors, see [LAN troubleshooting](/user-guide/troubleshooting/#lan-sync-cannot-connect-or-find-a-space).

## Stop sharing or unpair a device

- Turn off LAN sync in the Space settings to stop sharing that specific Space.
- Turn off **LAN Service** in **Settings → Devices** to stop all sharing. Device pairings remain saved.
- Select **Unpair** on either device to revoke access. Unpairing preserves downloaded files and history. Reconnecting requires a new pairing process.

## Use cloud sync

Cloud sync uses separate settings.
Sign in to your account in Eidos Lite Settings.
Open **Sync → Cloud Sync** and enable sync for your Space.
On another supported device, sign in to the same account and select the cloud Space.
Wait for the initial download to finish before you make changes.

Check your [account dashboard](https://eidos.space/account) for storage limits.
Canceling cloud sync does not delete your local files.

## Keep a separate backup

Sync transfers edits and deletions to other devices.
Always maintain an independent [backup](/user-guide/data-safety/).
