title: Use a Space on two devices
description: Enable optional Sync and check that changes reach the other device.

Sync transfers Space changes between devices through the cloud.
It requires an account and an eligible service plan.
Local files remain usable while offline or signed out.

## Connect the first device

1. Open the Space in Eidos Lite.
2. Sign in through Settings.
3. Open the Sync panel and enable Sync.
4. Wait for the panel to report completion or show an action you need to take.

Do not treat a connection indicator alone as proof that all changes arrived.
Review the panel's status before switching devices.

## Open the Space on another device

Sign in to the same account on the second device.
Use the option to sync an existing cloud Space, choose the Space, and let the
initial download finish.

**Check:** open a known file and confirm its contents before editing.

## Check a round trip

1. Make a small, recognizable edit on the first device.
2. Wait for Sync to complete.
3. Open the file on the second device and check the edit.

This confirms that the expected Space is connected on both devices.

## Work offline

Continue editing local files without a connection.
When the connection returns, check the Sync panel.
If Sync requires review, inspect the reported changes and follow the offered
resolution steps before continuing.

## Keep a separate backup

Sync moves changes; it can also move unwanted edits or deletions.
Keep an independent [backup](/user-guide/data-safety/).

Check the [account dashboard](https://eidos.space/account) for your current
plan and storage allowance. Losing access to Sync does not remove the
local files already on your device.
